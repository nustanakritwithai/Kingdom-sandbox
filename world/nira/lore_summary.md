# The Realm of Nira — Canon Geography, Fictional Lore

A fragmented maritime fantasy world whose peoples depend on bridges, ferries, mountain passes and seasonal trading fleets.

The Crown of Nira holds the central cultivated plains. In the north, Frostcrown guards ice roads and ancient shrines. Sunspire's caravan merchants cross desert and coastal channels. Elderwood's circle protects old trees; the Silver Ridge mines supply iron. Blossom mages and Azure scholars study elemental crystals. Marsh clans, redstone miners and free mariners dispute access to water, docks, minerals and routes. Beyond ordinary society, Inferno's citadel and Shadow Void's ritual structures threaten the network of settlements.

This is a *design narrative*, not a claim that the image contains labeled canon place names. All place markers and overland/sea routes are approximate and require art/navigation review.
